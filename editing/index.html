Working with me

Every show has its own rhythm.

The first episode is where I learn yours: how you talk, what you'd cut, where the good moments are. After that, every edit starts from your style, and by the third one you're sending fewer notes and approving faster.

You drop footage in a shared Google Drive folder (Dropbox works too), and I take it from there.
