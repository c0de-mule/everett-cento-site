About

If you keep hearing about AI and feeling like you're already behind, you're not.

You're right where most people are, and it's more approachable than anyone's making it sound.

I'm Everett. I help small businesses and solopreneurs put AI to work in a way that fits how they already run things. It's just me, no handoffs, no templated build, and I learn how you operate before I build anything.

My background's in Media Studies, not code, so I build systems around how you think and work, not how a textbook says one should look, and the good ones leave you more capable than they found you. Sometimes I build it for you, sometimes I teach you to build it yourself. Either way it's yours to run, and you'll know exactly how it works.

Most of the people I work with weren't sure any of this was for them when we started. Here's some of what we built together.
